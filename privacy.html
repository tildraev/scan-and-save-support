Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Scan & Save stores scanned documents and document information on your device by default. You do not need a Scan & Save account. Pro users may choose to turn on Private iCloud Sync to store their documents in their own iCloud account.

Camera and document processing

The camera is used when you choose to scan a document. Document capture, text recognition, editing, and suggested receipt details are processed on your device. Scans, preserved originals, recognized text, signatures, and document details are stored locally. We do not operate a document-processing server or send document contents to advertisers for targeting.

Sharing

When you use the Share button, the selected file is passed to the app or service you choose. That service’s privacy policy then applies.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app checks Apple-verified transactions to determine Pro access. We do not receive your payment-card or bank details. Purchases and refunds are managed through Apple.

iCloud Sync

You choose whether to turn on Private iCloud Sync. It stores documents, originals, recognized text, and document details in the app’s private database in your iCloud account so your devices can retrieve them. Apple processes this data under its privacy policy and iCloud terms, security controls, and storage limits. We do not receive your Apple Account password. Saved signatures and export presets are not synchronized by this feature.

Advertising and privacy choices

Free users may choose a rewarded video to unlock an additional scan session. The app integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent messages. Advertising services may process device or app identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad impressions and interactions, other product interactions, crash information, performance data, and other diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Their practices are described in Google’s privacy policy and Mobile Ads data disclosures.

The app requests non-personalized ads and disables Google’s publisher first-party identifier. When the app presents Apple’s tracking-permission prompt, you choose whether to allow our advertising partner to access the device’s advertising identifier for advertising measurement across apps and websites. Advertising starts after that choice is resolved. If you decline or tracking is restricted, Apple’s advertising identifier is unavailable and the app continues requesting non-personalized ads. Tracking permission is optional: the daily free scans, rewarded-video option, and Pro purchase remain available subject to the normal allowance and ad availability. You can review tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. These choices do not eliminate the other advertising data processing described above. Consent messages depend on your region and the applicable advertising configuration. When Google requires an entry point to revisit consent, open Settings → Privacy → Advertising privacy choices to review or change your choices. An additional scan is granted only after a rewarded video is completed; you can cancel or continue using the daily free allowance without watching a video.

Retention and deletion

Your saved documents remain until you delete them or remove the app’s local data. Deleting a document removes its local PDF, original, and saved document details. With sync enabled, deletion is also synchronized to your iCloud copy and other devices; small deletion markers are retained to prevent deleted documents from returning. Turning sync off does not delete existing local or cloud copies. To remove synchronized documents using the app, delete them while sync is enabled and let sync complete. Files you export to other apps or services must be managed separately in those destinations.

Local settings and saved signatures remain in the app’s data until changed or removed. Device or iCloud backups may retain data according to your Apple settings. Apple and Google control retention of data they process under their policies; deleting this app does not itself delete those providers’ records.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. Email is handled through Gmail and used to respond to your request and maintain necessary support records. Please avoid sending sensitive scans or credentials. Optional copied sync diagnostics contain app configuration and error codes; they exclude document names, contents, paths, record identifiers, and account identifiers. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of support correspondence, subject to any recordkeeping obligations.
