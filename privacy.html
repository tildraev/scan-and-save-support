Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Scan & Save stores scanned documents and document information on your device. We do not require an account and do not upload document content automatically.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document. Camera images are processed on the device and saved locally as PDFs.

Sharing

When you use the Share button, the selected file is passed to the app or service you choose. That service’s privacy policy then applies.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Scan & Save does not receive your payment details.

Advertising

Advertising is not enabled in the current release. If advertising is added, this policy and the consent experience will be updated before ads are shown.

Contact

For questions, use the support page.
