Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Scan & Save stores scanned documents and document information on your device by default. You do not need a Scan & Save account. Pro users may choose to turn on Private iCloud Sync to store their documents in their own iCloud account.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document. Camera images are processed on the device and saved locally as PDFs.

Sharing

When you use the Share button, the selected file is passed to the app or service you choose. That service’s privacy policy then applies.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Scan & Save does not receive your payment details.

iCloud Sync

You choose whether to turn on Private iCloud Sync. It stores documents privately in your iCloud account. Apple’s iCloud terms, security controls, and storage limits apply. Turning off sync does not delete the copies stored on your device.

Advertising

Free users may be offered a rewarded video to unlock an additional scan session. Advertising is provided through Google Mobile Ads and is subject to the consent choices shown by the app. Document content is not sent to advertisers for targeting.

Contact

For questions, email scanandsavesupport@gmail.com or visit Help & Support.
