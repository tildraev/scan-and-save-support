01 / GETTING STARTED

Scan & import

How do I scan a document?
  1. Tap Scan, or tap + and choose Scan with camera.
  2. Use Full page for documents, or Close-up for small text.
  3. Capture your pages and finish the scan.
  4. Review the suggested name, change it if you like, then tap Save.

Camera scanning needs a physical iPhone with camera access enabled.

Why is small text blurry when I hold the phone close?

Try Close-up at the top of the scanner. On supported iPhones, it uses the camera designed for nearby subjects. Tap the text to focus, hold steady, and use good lighting.

If the text is still blurry, move the phone back slightly. Close-up mode captures the whole photo; Full page mode handles automatic page detection.

Can I import an existing photo or PDF?

Tap + on the home screen, then choose Import photos or Import PDF or file. Select the files you want to bring into your library.

Why is my suggested scan name missing or incorrect?

The app reads text from the scan before suggesting a name. If it cannot find a useful name, you can enter one yourself or keep Untitled scan. You can also rename a saved scan by pressing and holding it, then choosing Rename.

02 / A PLACE FOR EVERYTHING

Folders & finding

How do I create a folder?

Folders are included with Pro. On the home screen, tap +, then New folder. Give it a name and tap Create. Open an existing folder first if you want the new folder inside it.

How do I move several scans or folders at once?
  1. Tap Edit beside the folder title.
  2. Select the scans or folders you want to move. You can use Select all.
  3. Tap Move and choose a destination.

To make a destination while moving, choose New folder, enter its name, choose where it belongs, then tap Create and move. The library opens the destination after a successful move.

What do All scans and Unfiled mean?

All scans shows every document in your library. Unfiled shows documents that have not been assigned to a folder.

In Edit mode, selecting All scans selects every document; selecting Unfiled selects only documents without a folder. These built-in views stay available after their contents are moved or deleted.

How do I find text inside a scan?

Use Search scans on the home screen. Search checks document names, folder names, tags, and readable text found inside your scans. A search covers the whole library, even when you start inside a folder.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS

Signatures

How do I create a signature?

With Pro, open a document, tap the sparkle menu, choose Pro tools, then Create saved signature. You can draw your signature or choose Type to enter your name and preview different styles. Tap Save to keep it on your device.

You can also create a signature from the page editor when placing one.

How do I place or resize a signature?
  1. Open the document and tap the pencil.
  2. Tap the signature icon beside the page.
  3. Drag the signature into position. Pinch or drag its corner to resize it.
  4. Tap Apply, then Save in the page editor.

A saved signature can be reused on another document.

04 / FINISHING TOUCHES

Editing & cleanup

How do I change a page’s color, brightness, or contrast?

Open a document, tap the pencil, then the sliders icon beside a page. Adjust the controls, then tap Apply in the top-right corner. Tap Save in the page editor to keep your changes.

How does Clean Up work?

In the page editor, tap the wand icon. Select a mark on plain paper, then tap Preview cleanup. The tool fills the selection with a nearby paper color, or white if you choose Use white paper.

Use Undo to step back, Save to accept the preview, or Cancel to discard it. Then save the page editor.

Anything inside the selection is erased. Clean Up cannot reconstruct text or artwork hidden beneath a mark.

Can I zoom in while cleaning up?

Yes. Pinch to zoom, move the page with two fingers, and drag with one finger to select an area. Tap Fit page to see the whole page again.

What happens when I delete pages?

Page deletions are saved immediately. If you delete the last page, the app asks whether you want to delete the entire document.

You can restore pages removed from an existing document with Revert to original. Deleting the entire document also removes its saved original.

05 / A FRESH START

Original copies

How do I revert to the original document?

Open the document, tap the sparkle menu, choose Revert to original, and confirm. This restores the original pages, including available color and pages removed during editing. Later page edits, signatures, and cleanup are removed. The document’s name and folder stay the same.

Can I get color back after saving a scan as grayscale?

If the saved original contains color, Revert to original brings it back. New scans preserve the camera-provided images before the app’s color filter; imports preserve their source.

For documents saved before original-copy preservation was added, the earliest available version is the one saved at that time. Color already discarded from that version cannot be recovered.

Does keeping an original mean I can restore a deleted document?

No. The original is kept alongside a document for reverting edits. Deleting the entire document removes both copies. Keep a separate copy of documents you need to retain, and check that any cloud synchronization has completed before relying on it.

06 / THE OTHER DETAILS

Pro, sharing & privacy

What happens when I use my free scan allowance?

The free plan includes two scan sessions per day, with up to ten pages per session. The limit prompt offers Watch a video, Unlock Pro, and Cancel. A rewarded video can provide another session when a video is available.

How do I unlock Pro or restore a purchase?

Open Settings → Scan & Save Pro. Choose Unlock Pro to view the available purchase, or Restore Purchase if you already bought Pro using your Apple Account.

If the app says purchases are not available yet, please check back later or contact support. Your existing scans remain available.

How do I share a PDF or use saved export settings?

Open a scan and tap the Share icon to send its PDF to an app or location you choose. Pro’s Advanced Export offers additional format and quality settings.

Run saved export recipe reuses the format and image quality you saved in Advanced Export, then opens the sharing menu. It does not send a document by itself.

Where are my scans stored?

Documents are saved on your device. Pro includes iCloud features that use your private iCloud account when configured and available. Sharing a scan passes the selected file to the app or service you choose.

You do not need a Scan & Save account. See our privacy policy for more information.
